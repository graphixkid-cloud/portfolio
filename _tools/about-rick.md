## Verizon — more detail on the consumer portfolio
Rick directed UX strategy for a $1.25B Verizon consumer portfolio reaching 20M+ subscribers.
- Verizon Family / Smart Family ($220M revenue): consolidated fragmented parent and child apps into one Verizon Family experience; redesigned location tracking, parental controls, driving insights and trusted contacts; addressed friction in pairing and permissions. Results: combined NPS rose from −17 to +2 within a year (+7 on iOS), net churn turned negative (−2.47%), app store ratings reached 4.4–4.8 stars, and subscriptions passed 950K (about 1.2M users).
- Gizmo Watch and GizmoHub: smartwatch for children aged about 6–11 with safe communication and no open internet. Rick designed on-watch touch interactions, circular navigation and the GizmoHub parent app, across Gizmo Watch 2, Gizmo Watch 3 and Gizmo Watch 3 Adventure. Scale: about 700K active kids' watches and 2.4M contacts. Awards: GOOD DESIGN Award (2019) and Red Dot Design Award (2023).
- Care Smart: older adults were triggering emergency alerts by accident. Rick's team tested directly with older adults and family caregivers and replaced the single-tap emergency trigger with an intentional slider, cutting accidental alerts without slowing down real emergencies. The platform later expanded to Apple Watch and Samsung Galaxy Watch4+.
- 5G Home Internet ($514M revenue, 5.7M users): channel-neutral customer journeys for 5G Home Internet and 5G Home Voice, including mesh router onboarding and out-of-box setup.
- Verizon Cloud ($1B revenue, 5.74M users): unified product roadmaps across mobile and desktop to simplify backup and onboarding.
- Verizon Protect, Digital Secure and the security dashboard ($17.7M revenue for Verizon Protect): integrated conversational support through the Security Advisor chatbot, aligned with Verizon Assistant.
- Design systems: Rick led adoption of Verizon Design System (VDS) 3.0 for Verizon Family.

## EBS BrokerTec (now CME Group) — more detail
- EBS Direct served 10,000+ institutional bank traders, with usability work in London and New York.
- Iceberg orders: traders slice, hide and execute large-block spot orders through high-density tiles and keyboard hotkeys.
- Errors: manual ticket entry was replaced with single-click quote responses, and pre-trade credit checks were built into the order ticket, removing the source of multi-million-dollar "fat-finger" errors.
